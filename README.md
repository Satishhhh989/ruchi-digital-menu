##  Experience Features
- **Bilingual Experience (English & ಕನ್ನಡ):** Seamless one-tap language switch (`[ EN | ಕನ್ನಡ ]`) translates the entire menu in-place — all 153 dishes, categories, page titles, subtitles, dietary tags, and navigation with native Karnataka culinary terminology and flawless orthography.
- **Dual-Script Search:** Search dishes instantly in either English or Kannada (e.g. searching "Paneer" or "ಪನ್ನೀರ್" reveals all 35 paneer specialities).
- **Physical Book Feel:** Dedicated luxury leather front cover, stitched spine, gold foil stamping, and warm archival ivory paper pages.
- **Mobile-First Responsive Layout:** Perfectly proportioned for portrait smartphone displays (320px–480px) with zero horizontal clipping.
- **Page-Turning Motion:** Natural horizontal swipe gestures and smooth slide-and-fold transitions.
- **Desktop Spread:** Realistic two-page spread with central binding crease on desktop screens.
- **Integrated Navigation:** Discreet chapter table of contents and full-text search across all 153 official dishes.
- **100% Verified Menu Dataset:** Complete North Indian and Marwadi vegetarian selections with preserved prices and categories.
